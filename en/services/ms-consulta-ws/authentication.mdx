Every route in the service requires the **`Api-Key`** header, with a single exception: `GET /health`.

```bash
curl "https://<HOST>/api/entidades/edomex/obtenerReporte/<ID>" \
  -H "Api-Key: <API_KEY>"
```

## What the service validates

Each call is evaluated against four controls, in order:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Authentication (Api-Key)">
    The credential is validated against the consumer registry. An inactive, expired, or unknown key produces `401 Unauthorized`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Roles">
    Each route declares the roles it accepts. The existing roles are `entity`, `admin`, `superadmin`, `system`, `entitySimplifiedService`, and `Reporte Completo WS`. Legacy entity routes generally require `superadmin` or `Reporte Completo WS`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Policies">
    Policies (`WebService`, `Servicio Simplificado`) scope the type of service the consumer has contracted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Distributor">
    Each route accepts only its entity's distributors (plus `Local`). The distributor is derived from the `Api-Key`: it doesn't travel in the request.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  There are no `userid`, `reporteid`, or `distributor` headers in this service: the consumer's entire identity is derived from the `Api-Key`.
</Note>

## Dealers: slug validation

The `/api/dealers/{dealer}` routes add their own validation: the `Api-Key`'s distributor must be flagged as a dealer and the path slug must be its own. One dealer's key cannot operate under another dealer's slug.

## Credential security

- The `Api-Key` is **masked** in logs and stored as a hash in the audit trail: the full value is never recorded (ADR-0004).
- The `401` always responds with a fixed message, with no detail about the reason, to avoid leaking credential information.
- Rotate your credential immediately if you suspect exposure; request the rotation from the CARFAX MX team.

## Audit and correlation

Every transaction creates an audit record whose identifier (`_id`) travels in the response. Keep it: it's the folio CARFAX MX uses to trace your transaction end to end. The optional `x-correlation-id` header is propagated to logs and traces.
